## Store Listing Text

### Short Description (132 chars max)
White-label content, merchant research & price suggestions in your browser. No credentials bundled.

### Detailed Description
SI Content & Commerce Agent brings content import, merchant price research, and privacy hygiene to your browser — no WordPress required.

**Features:**
• RSS/X.com feed import with deduplication
• AI enrichment via your OpenAI-compatible endpoint
• Merchant research: Google News RSS, Google CSE, DeepSeek, or your MCP bridge
• Price suggestions with undercut/margin floor
• SEO checklist: Yoast meta, schema, canonical links
• Privacy hygiene: remove zero-width, bidi, exotic spaces
• Campaign CSV analysis (offline, no account needed)
• Manifest V3, no remote code, minimal permissions

**You control everything:** endpoints, keys, tokens, sync intervals. Nothing is bundled or hardcoded.
